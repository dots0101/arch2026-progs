# Authorship and AI assistance disclosure

ChatGPT helped prepare the deliverable on 2026-11-12. Its contributions
included retrieving the assignment and framework, porting the earlier
user-provided H48 solver, drafting C and RV32I code, running builds and
tests, collecting measurements, and preparing the report and reproduction
scripts. The student confirmed that they revised the RV32I assembly in
the delivered package. The exact scope of those revisions remains to be
documented. The numeric assembly tables come from the existing C values.

The earlier H48 source and data were retrieved from the user's existing
`RUBIK_N2_H48_ASSIGNMENT_SAFE_FINAL_20261003.zip` archive. Its SHA-256 is
`ce6a4e322acd1c9efdcf4b9bed28dcf407e02e212962794446b9f5a104b76cb4`.
The archive establishes the provenance of the reused solver and tables.

Commits created by ChatGPT during preparation identify ChatGPT as their
author and retain the supplied commit hooks and hash chain.

The course requires students to author the designated handwritten work,
disclose assistance, and complete the separate interview under its
in-session rules. [Course AI guidelines](https://hackmd.io/@sysprog/arch2026-ai-guidelines).
